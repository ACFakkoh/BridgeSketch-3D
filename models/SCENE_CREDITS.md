# Trains and buildings

The three selectable train consists use models from [Kenney Train Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/train-kit). Diesel freight uses `train-diesel-a` and two `train-carriage-box` wagons. Bullet and city passenger trains each use their matching A, B and C cars.

Urban scenery uses four models: `building-type-a` and `building-type-g` from [Kenney City Kit Suburban](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban), and `building-a` and `building-j` from [Kenney City Kit Commercial](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial).

All three packs are created by Kenney and released under [Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The original licence text from each pack is retained alongside this file. The selected OBJ meshes were converted to local ES-module geometry, scaled to the concept scene, and paired with converted palette atlases. They require no runtime network access.
