Road profile

A crest keeps equal elevations at both ends.

Road section

Right-hand traffic. Cycle bridges may be 3–6 m wide with one 1.5–2 m cycle lane; 0 lanes gives a pedestrian-only deck. Remaining width forms outer shoulders.

Outer shoulder · L / R—
Crossfall

The crown line is the profile grade line (0 = deck centreline, + to the right). Slab, barriers, girders and approaches follow the crossfall; girders are set at their own level under it.

Centre median & line

Centred in the road after side sidewalks. Barrier height 1.10 m; island height 200 mm. At least one lane per direction.

Sidewalks

200 mm raised surface, 280 mm thick with a 1 % crossfall to the road. Left/right looking from bridge start to end.

Bridge railings

Steel railings sit on 450 × 280 mm wheel curbs (road face 35 mm in 280 mm); posts at 3 m on one grid along the bridge and approaches. The bridge railings always continue to the end of the return walls. The architectural railing leans 12° outwards, white-grey.

Approach barriers

Pedestrian bridges continue with a wheel curb and 20C railing, or nothing.

Night lighting

Lights switch on from 20:00 to 06:30. Street lights on 10 m poles default to 30 m; handrail LEDs under the top rail of 20C or SDC railings default to 3 m.

Centre spacing—

Overhang: outer girder centre to deck edge. Girder deck: 200, 225 or 250 mm concrete slab + 65 mm asphalt on the roadway, 50 mm haunches over the top flanges. Solid slab thickness uses its own depth.

Depth & continuity

Smooth soffit with 400 mm constant depth over bearings. Single spans deepen at abutments.

Concrete finish
Steel finish

Plate flanges 500 × 50 mm · webs and stiffeners 14 mm · cross-frames at 8 m or less, deep diaphragms at supports. Steel boxes have two 500 × 50 mm top flanges and inclined side webs.

Pier type

One pier arrangement for all interior supports. Bearings 75 mm; level plinths vary with the deck profile (100 mm minimum).

Columns

Outer column spacing is centre to centre (0 = automatic, 64 % of the deck); inner columns are equally spaced.

Pier cap

Width along the road; thickness vertically. Cap length follows the deck width. Taper start: 0 = outer column face.

Front slope & cones

2H:1V embankments. Front cones can project ahead of the abutment plane; stone facing is limited to the cone segments.

Crossings by span

Clearance moves the crossing elevation; subsequent bridge edits recalculate it.

Terrain

Natural valley: approach embankments. Follows the road profile: a cut valley. Terrain width is measured across the scene (140 m by default; curved approaches may need more). 2H:1V embankments with quarter-cone ends.

Surroundings

Trees and buildings stay outside the bridge corridor and the area between crossing roads or railways. Change the seed for another layout.

Sky & weather

Stylised gradient is tuned for golden hour. Haze is tinted by the hour; rain and snow always add fog.

Rendering

Auto picks from the GPU and lowers itself if the frame rate drops. Performance: no water reflection. Balanced: half-resolution reflection. High: dense meadow, sharp reflection. Glow applies to Balanced and High.

BridgeSketch 3D

A quick visual tool for bridge concepts.

By Anthony Chéruel

Starfield and grass species adapted from CK42BB/procedural-stars-threejs and procedural-grass-threejs (MIT); physical sky after Tw1ddle/Sky-Shader and the three.js Sky object (MIT).

Version 0.6.1

New pier shapes (rounded or pointed wall ends, flared hammerheads, inclined portals, V piers), a light bloom, faster parameter edits, clear-coat vehicles with contact shadows and a realistic paint mix, and gull-like birds. Folded parameter groups with a value summary, a reorganised Site tab, formulas in the numeric fields (for example =2*17.4 or (12+3)/2), a File menu with Open, Save, Undo and Redo (Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y), a modified marker, a local autosave with a restore offer, and an option to turn the birds off.

Version 0.6.0

Crowned or uniformly sloped deck, continuous girders by default, NEBT intermediate diaphragms and full-width haunches, girder screens, linear cap tapers, the Samuel-De Champlain architectural railing, a 3 m post grid and railings continued over the return walls, crowned and ditched crossing roads (one-way pairs), open-ground spans, a double truck, a seeded starfield, three grass species, concrete finishes, a high-quality PNG snapshot and a phone layout.

Version 0.5.6

Time runs by default (15 min every 5 s, stop with ❚❚), golden hour before sunset (19:00), 1–4 concrete boxes under one deck, a meandering river in the profile-following terrain, Orbit off by default, Stats at the bottom centre.

Version 0.5.5

New structural systems: prestressed concrete box girders, rigid frames (portique), strutted frames with inclined legs, concrete or steel deck arches and tied arches. MTQ Type 311 barrier and 311 A+B steel rail, 12 m street lights with a single davit arm, autumn scenery with falling leaves, moving cloud shadows, pier water marks and a terrain that can follow the road profile. Faster rendering: Balanced by default, an Auto quality, tree and grass levels of detail, on-demand shadows and a Stats overlay.

Concept visualization only. This tool does not perform structural design or verify code compliance.
