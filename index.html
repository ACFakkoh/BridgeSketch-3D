BridgeSketch 3D

A quick visual tool for bridge concepts.

By Anthony Chéruel

Version 0.4.0

Golden-hour orbit, live time-of-day shadows, six bridge concepts, longer 4–12-car trains and moving traffic, locally bundled Kenney vehicles, trains and buildings, textured rivers, adjustable lane and bent sizes, continuous lane lines, detailed steel bracing, painted and weathered finishes, and a richer meadow. Includes the obstacle-side tour, export tools and offline/GitHub Pages packages.

Concept visualization only. This tool does not perform structural design or verify code compliance.
